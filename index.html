Pharmacology Q 47 / 200

A client is prescribed metformin for type 2 diabetes. Which lab value requires the nurse to hold the medication and notify the provider?

A
HbA1c of 8.2%
B
Serum creatinine of 2.1 mg/dL
C
Fasting glucose of 180 mg/dL
D
eGFR of 65 mL/min
✦
NurseIQ explains your mistake

You selected A (HbA1c), which suggests you're focused on glycemic control — but metformin's risk is lactic acidosis via renal accumulation. An elevated creatinine (B) signals impaired renal clearance, requiring the medication to be held. eGFR of 65 is acceptable; the threshold is typically <30.
